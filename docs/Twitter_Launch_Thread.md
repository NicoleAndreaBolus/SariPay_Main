### 🧵 Tweet 2 (The Problem & Solution)

1/ 📦 **The Problem:** 
Informal retail supply chains face massive friction. Merchants risk upfront payments for missing goods, while suppliers risk non-payment on Cash-on-Delivery (COD).

2/ 🔒 **The Solution:** 
SariPay locks stablecoin payments into a Soroban smart contract escrow upon order placement. Payout is released to the supplier in <5s when the merchant scans the delivery QR code!
